## Three Prioritized Recommendations
1. **Re‑evaluate Pricing Strategy** – With 17 negative mentions, price is the biggest pain point. Consider adjusting the price, offering flat‑rate or reduced shipping, and ensuring the advertised quantity matches the cost to improve perceived value.  
2. **Enhance Taste Quality** – Taste received 13 negative mentions. Focus on recipe improvements, flavor intensity, and preventing dents or damage during transit that can affect flavor perception.  
3. **Address Health‑Related Concerns** – Health issues (8 negatives) include efficacy and product quality (e.g., bamboo pieces, detox claims). Strengthen quality control, verify ingredient potency, and ensure product descriptions accurately reflect health benefits.
